Investigate a pattern, not a person

  1. Observe the activity. Each dot is a payment record. Multiple payments between the same endpoints share one line. The line is not a count of transactions.
  2. Read the clock and time window. The last-hour view removes older payments as the replay clock advances. A falling count does not mean payments were deleted, money vanished or an account was cleared. Choose all history to keep arrived records visible.
  3. Inspect the alert. A rule identifies a pattern worth reviewing. Select its card to see counted payments, supporting context and the trigger time.
  4. Inspect the account. Review incoming/outgoing totals, recorded counterparties and rule explanations. Missing owner details and unknown counterparties are explicitly shown as unavailable.
  5. Record a review status. Use Watch, Under review, Escalated for review or Review closed. A closed review is not a finding that activity is safe. Add a note explaining your reasoning.
  6. Follow up through authorised processes. A real institution needs verified records, an analyst workflow and appropriate access controls. This demo does not file reports or block money.

What sound means

Enable sound yourself. A new alert during forward replay plays a soft tone. If a burst reaches 12 payments within an hour or an account gains a second distinct rule, two higher tones signal additional review priority. Rewinding, filtering and selecting accounts do not sound an alarm. The visual announcement always remains available. Priorities here are a transparent demo policy, not a validated risk model.

About review notes

Marks and notes are stored for this synthetic dataset in this browser where storage is available. They are not synced, authenticated or an audit trail. Do not enter real personal or financial information. Export notes if you need a copy.

READ THE NETWORK

Connections with evidence.
Uncertainty made visible.

Accounts are points. Payments are paths.

Arrowheads show direction; thicker paths carry greater total value. The account selector and time window reduce clutter. Multiple payments between the same endpoints share a path. Each small point on a path represents a recorded payment. Select a point or transaction ID to inspect it. Its position along the path is for spacing, not time or geography. Playback advances the clock by seconds, minutes or hours. Press “Next payment” to skip quiet periods.

Unknown means unknown.

Bank accounts use AC identifiers; external beneficiaries use EX identifiers. Unidentified sources and recipients remain grouped placeholders. This dataset does not establish a bank-wide ownership graph or locate criminals.

Four rules, traceable evidence.

Every completed alert reconciles to the existing PostgreSQL AML rules. During replay, each alert version contains only evidence available at the displayed UTC time. Supporting transactions are distinguished from the transactions included in the alert amount. Repeated use of evidence across flags means alert values must not be added as unique suspicious volume.

The proposed bank-facing value is traceable review: payment → rule → evidence → analyst decision. This is a product hypothesis to validate with institutions, not a claim of unique market capability. Research prototype, synthetic data only. No bank connectivity, payment blocking, shared case management or production detection claim. The full local analytics interface and setup are available in the repository.
