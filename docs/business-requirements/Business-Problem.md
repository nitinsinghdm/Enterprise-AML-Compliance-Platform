# Business Problem

## Background

NorthStar is a fictional multinational financial institution operating across Europe and Asia.  The bank provides retail, commercial, and corporate banking services while processing millions of financial transactions each day.

As regulatory requirements continue to evolve, financial institutions must comply with Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Know Your Customer (KYC), sanctions screening, and customer due diligence regulations. Failure to comply with these regulations can result in significant financial penalties, reputational damage, and increased operational risk.

To strengthen its compliance capabilities, NorthStar Bank has initiated a Compliance Analytics Transformation programme aimed at modernising its monitoring and reporting processes through scalable data engineering, predictive analytics, and business intelligence.
