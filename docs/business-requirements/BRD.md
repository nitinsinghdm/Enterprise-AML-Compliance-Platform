# Business Requirements Document

## 1. Executive Summary

NorthStar Bank is a fictional multinational financial institution providing retail, commercial, and corporate banking services across Europe and Asia. As the bank continues to expand, the volume of customer accounts, financial transactions, and regulatory obligations has increased significantly.

The existing compliance process relies primarily on rule-based monitoring systems, resulting in a high volume of false positive alerts and extensive manual investigations by compliance analysts. This impacts operational efficiency, increases investigation costs, and delays the identification of genuinely suspicious activities.

The objective of this project is to design and develop an enterprise-grade Compliance Analytics Platform capable of supporting Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Know Your Customer (KYC), sanctions screening, customer risk scoring, and executive reporting. The platform will leverage scalable data engineering, cloud data warehousing, machine learning, and business intelligence technologies to improve compliance operations and provide actionable insights for decision-makers.
